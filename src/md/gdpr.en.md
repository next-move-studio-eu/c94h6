# GDPR / Privacy

## Offline application

This editor runs **locally on your machine**. It does **not** send any content, user data, or personal data over the internet.

- Your documents and files stay on your device.
- No analytics, tracking, or telemetry are used.
- No account or login is required.

We do not process personal data in the sense of the GDPR because the application does not collect or transmit any such data.
